# Security

graft is a small solo project and has no bug bounty, but reports are welcome and read.

To report a vulnerability, open a private security advisory on this repository (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability"). Please do not open a public issue for it. Include what you did, what you expected and what happened; a proof of concept helps.

What is in scope and what the service already guards against (abuse limits, upload checks, the operator surface, the threat model) is written down in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), under "Abuse guardrails" and "Security". Known weak points are listed there under "Known debt".
